This is the very first blog post. You can edit this text directly in your browser.

## What to Try

Here are some things you can do with the editor:

- Change any heading or paragraph
- Move blocks up and down
- Insert new paragraphs between existing ones
- Delete blocks that aren't needed

Each change is saved immediately — no separate save button needed. Just click outside the block or click another block to trigger the save.
